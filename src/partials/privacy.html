Privacy

Transparency is part of reliable legal information.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Workers with static website files. Cloudflare processes the technical information needed to deliver and protect website traffic under its own service terms and privacy policy. The office has not configured a client database or custom analytics system on this website.

Contact information

Visitors may choose to contact the office by telephone, email or WhatsApp. Those communications are handled by the relevant telephone, email or messaging provider and are subject to that provider’s terms and privacy practices. The website does not currently provide an online enquiry form, appointment calendar, client portal or secure document upload.

Confidential information

Do not submit case files, identity documents, passwords, bank information or sensitive client details through ordinary email or WhatsApp unless the office has first confirmed an appropriate secure method. A general enquiry does not create a lawyer-client relationship.

Questions and updates

The law office should review and approve this notice, including how it handles enquiries, before treating it as its final privacy policy. Contact the office through the details on the Contact page for privacy questions.

Contact

For corrections or questions about a source, send a non-confidential enquiry.
